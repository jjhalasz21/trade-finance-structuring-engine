# Permian Pure Energy – Trade Finance Solution
*HSBC Global Trade Solutions | January 2025*

---

## Executive Summary

- **Problem:** Permian Pure Energy carries $190mm in payables across 85 suppliers with a 45-day payment cycle, creating a $150mm working capital gap that constrains operational flexibility in a volatile E&P market.
- **HSBC Proposal:** Supply Chain Finance (SCF) program enabling Permian to extend supplier payment terms from 45 to 75 days while suppliers receive immediate cash at a discount, funded by HSBC against Permian's anchor credit.
- **Outcome:** $152mm facility closes the working capital gap, improves cash conversion cycle by 30 days, and keeps trade payables off the balance sheet—no covenant impact, no equity dilution.

---

## Client Diagnostic

Permian Pure Energy operates in upstream E&P with $1,200mm in annual revenue and a highly fragmented supplier base of 85 vendors spanning oilfield services, midstream gathering, and chemicals. The company currently pays suppliers on 45-day terms but has identified a strategic opportunity to extend Days Payable Outstanding (DPO) to 75 days—a 30-day extension typical of larger integrated operators. This extension would unlock $150mm in working capital relief without raising debt or equity. The absence of meaningful accounts receivable ($0mm) and inventory ($0mm) rules out Receivables Finance and Inventory Finance; SCF is the only product that directly addresses Permian's payables-driven working capital need while preserving balance sheet classification.

---

## Proposed Solution

**Supply Chain Finance (SCF) – $152mm Uncommitted Facility**

HSBC will establish an SCF program allowing Permian to extend payment terms to suppliers while HSBC advances 100% of irrevocably confirmed invoices to participating suppliers at a discount. Suppliers receive cash within 5 business days; Permian pays HSBC on the original extended terms (up to 75 days). This structure is ideal for Permian because it (1) closes the $150mm working capital gap without on-balance-sheet debt, (2) accommodates the 85-supplier base with minimal operational friction, and (3) leverages Permian's investment-grade customer relationships (Dominion Energy, Targa Resources) as credit anchors for the program.

## Structure Diagram

```
Permian Pure Energy (Buyer)
        │
        ├─ Issues PO to Supplier
        │
        └─ Supplier delivers goods/services
                │
                ├─ Supplier issues invoice (75-day terms)
                │
                └─ Supplier submits invoice to HSBC SCF platform
                        │
                        ├─ HSBC verifies irrevocable confirmation
                        │
                        └─ HSBC advances 100% to Supplier (Day 0)
                                │
                                └─ Supplier receives cash, discount applied
                                        │
                                        └─ Permian pays HSBC on Day 75
                                                │
                                                └─ HSBC settlement
```

**Cash Flow Timeline:**
- **Day 0:** Supplier submits confirmed invoice to HSBC.
- **Day 1–5:** HSBC advances 100% of invoice value to supplier's account.
- **Day 75:** Permian remits payment to HSBC (extended DPO achieved).
- **Benefit:** Permian extends payables by 30 days; supplier gets immediate liquidity.
