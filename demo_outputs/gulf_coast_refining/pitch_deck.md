## Executive Summary

- **The Problem:** Gulf Coast Refining carries $320mm in receivables against a $200mm working capital gap, with 65% of AR concentrated in five investment-grade obligors (Shell AA, Pilot A, Delta/Marathon/Phillips 66 BBB). - **HSBC's Proposal:** A $250mm committed, non-recourse Receivables Finance facility structured for true-sale derecognition under IFRS 9 / ASC 860, leveraging the high-quality obligor base to achieve an 86.5% advance rate and tight pricing of SOFR + 145bps + 25bps fee.

- **What You Get:** $187.5mm in immediate liquidity at first drawdown, off-balance-sheet accounting treatment, and a committed funding source that closes your working capital gap while preserving balance sheet capacity for growth or strategic initiatives.

## Client Diagnostic

Gulf Coast Refining operates in downstream refining with $4.0bn in annual revenue and a 30-day payment cycle. Your $320mm AR balance represents 8% of revenue—typical for the sector—but the concentration is exceptional: your top five obligors account for 65% of receivables, all rated BBB or better. This is a strength, not a weakness. The obligor quality (Shell Trading US at AA, Pilot Flying J at A, and three BBB-rated majors) makes your receivables highly bankable for securitization or true-sale treatment. Your explicit off-balance-sheet appetite and $200mm working capital gap indicate you need funding that doesn't consume balance sheet capacity. Receivables Finance is the natural fit.

---

## Proposed Solution

**Product:** Committed, Non-Recourse Receivables Finance Facility

**Facility Size:** $250mm

**Rationale:** Your AR pool is $304mm eligible (after concentration and tenor filters). A $250mm facility at 86.5% advance rate monetizes $216mm of that pool, delivering $187.5mm in liquidity at first drawdown. Because your obligors are investment-grade and concentrated in creditworthy counterparties, we can structure this as a true-sale transaction with no recourse to you—meaning the receivables transfer off your balance sheet under IFRS 9 / ASC 860. This achieves your off-balance-sheet objective while providing committed funding certainty to close your $200mm working capital gap.
